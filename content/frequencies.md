---
title: Seven Frequency Waves
description: The Prism Spectrum of Visible Light
---

When pure white light passes through a prism, it fractures into the seven distinct frequencies of the visible spectrum. Each frequency represents a fundamental wavelength of universal energy, carrying its own physical and spiritual signature.

::runic-divider{label="The Prism Spectrum" icon="i-lucide-rainbow"}
::

::div{class="flex flex-col gap-6 w-full not-prose mb-8"}
  ::hud-panel{title="First Wave: Red" status="nominal" color="red" icon="i-lucide-radio-receiver"}
    **The Longest Wavelength / Dense Energy (~620–750 nm | ~400–484 THz).** Red forms the absolute foundation of the visible spectrum. Experiencing the lowest frequency and longest wavelength, it penetrates deepest into the physical world, scattering the least in our atmosphere.
    
    It represents strong grounding, raw passion, primal instinct, and the visceral life force that literally roots us into material existence. Red is the anchor of the spectrum, pulling us firmly into the present moment.
  ::

  ::hud-panel{title="Second Wave: Orange" status="nominal" color="orange" icon="i-lucide-flame"}
    **The Wave of Vitality / Kinetic Warmth (~590–620 nm | ~484–508 THz).** As the wavelength shortens slightly, energy rapidly accelerates into Orange. This frequency vibrates with enthusiasm, warmth, and an undeniable kinetic joy.
    
    Orange serves directly as the driving force of curiosity, gut intuition, and expansive creativity. It is the bright frequency of the sunrise, representing awakening, active energy, and the playful outward exploration of the physical realm.
  ::

  ::hud-panel{title="Third Wave: Yellow" status="nominal" color="yellow" icon="i-lucide-sun"}
    **The Wave of Clarity / Radiant Intellect (~570–590 nm | ~508–526 THz).** Approaching the middle frequencies, Yellow shines with brilliant, unmistakable clarity. Structurally, it is highly stimulating to the optical senses and the neural pathways of the intellect.
    
    Yellow embodies logic, sudden illumination, and razor-sharp conscious awareness. It is the solar spark that cuts immediately through mental confusion, giving structured brightness and deep focus to the mind.
  ::

  ::hud-panel{title="Fourth Wave: Green" status="nominal" color="green" icon="i-lucide-leaf"}
    **The Center Wave / Harmonic Balance (~495–570 nm | ~526–606 THz).** Green systematically sits in the exact middle of the visible spectrum. It acts as the perfect fulcrum bridge, balancing the slower, dense terrestrial frequencies with the faster, ethereal cosmic frequencies.
    
    It naturally resonates with profound cellular harmony, biological growth, and natural equilibrium. Green invites powerful healing, representing the lush, thriving interconnectedness of all living things actively sustained within the Sacred Realm.
  ::

  ::hud-panel{title="Fifth Wave: Blue" status="nominal" color="blue" icon="i-lucide-waves"}
    **The Cool Expanse / Tranquil Resonance (~450–495 nm | ~606–668 THz).** As pure energy crosses into the higher, faster frequencies, it formally transitions into the calming expanse of Blue. This short wavelength is peaceful, deeply expansive, and steady in its continuous vibration.
    
    Blue visually represents the infinite calm, from the atmosphere of the sky to the crushing depth of the ocean. It continuously conveys absolute truth, serene clarity, and the quiet depth completely necessary for authentic communication and contemplation.
  ::

  ::hud-panel{title="Sixth Wave: Indigo" status="nominal" color="indigo" icon="i-lucide-moon"}
    **The Wave of Depth / Inner Vision (~425–450 nm | ~668–714 THz).** Rapidly moving towards the highest thresholds of human optical recognition, Indigo carries a mysterious, deeply penetrating energy. It historically holds the liminal frequency of twilight and deep midnight space.
    
    Indigo actively stimulates inner perception and clairvoyant intuition. It is the complex synthesis of deep structural blue and highly energetic violet, effectively cutting through physical illusions to explicitly reveal the underlying cosmic architecture.
  ::

  ::hud-panel{title="Seventh Wave: Violet" status="nominal" color="violet" icon="i-lucide-sparkles"}
    **The Shortest Wavelength / Cosmic Frequency (~380–425 nm | ~714–789 THz).** Violet vibrates visibly at the absolute highest frequency and correspondingly shortest wavelength of the entire visible spectrum. It stands right at the precise boundary line between the visible physical world and the invisible ultraviolet dimension beyond.
    
    It signifies ultimate spiritual transcendence, regality, and the final dissolution of the individual ego directly into the unified cosmic consciousness. Violet is the exact point where physical light forcefully returns to the divine infinite source.
  ::
::

To understand the Prism Spectrum is to recognize that everything in the Sacred Realm - every note, every feeling, every atom - is merely light vibrating at a specific sacred frequency.
