# Days Organization

The Sacred Realm organizes the seven days of the week starting from Tuesday, reflecting the dynamic, action-oriented energy of Mars as the initiation of the celestial cycle.

## Celestial Rhythm

1. **Tuesday: Mars** (The Day of Fire and Action / Wave 1)
2. **Wednesday: Mercury** (The Day of Wind and Intellect / Wave 2)
3. **Sunday: The Sun** (The Day of Light and Vitality / Wave 3)
4. **Friday: Venus** (The Day of Spirit and Harmony / Wave 4)
5. **Thursday: Jupiter** (The Day of Thunder and Expansion / Wave 5)
6. **Saturday: Saturn** (The Day of Time and Structure / Wave 6)
7. **Monday: The Moon** (The Day of Tides and Reflection / Wave 7)

## Alignment

This organization influences both the **7 Days** lexicon and the **7 Sages** quests, ensuring that the week begins with the stoking of inner fire and concludes with lunar reflection.
